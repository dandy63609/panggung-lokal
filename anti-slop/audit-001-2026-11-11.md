## Numbered Findings List

### 1. Viewport Meta Configuration Missing Safe Area and Keyboard Attributes
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / R-39 (Accessible Semantics & App Shell)
- **Files:** `public/index.html`, `public/search.html`, `public/profile.html`, `public/login.html`, `public/register.html`, `public/dashboard-band.html`, `public/dashboard-organizer.html`, `public/admin.html`, `public/forgot-password.html`, `public/reset-password.html`
- **Reason:** All 10 HTML files lack `viewport-fit=cover` (preventing safe area insets `env(safe-area-inset-*)` from working) and `interactive-widget=resizes-content` (preventing viewport resizing when mobile virtual keyboard opens).

---

### 2. Missing Mobile Browser Theme Color Meta Tags
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / R-39 (Accessible Semantics & App Shell)
- **Files:** `public/*.html` (All 10 HTML files)
- **Reason:** Missing `<meta name="theme-color">` for light and dark schemes causes the mobile status bar and browser chrome to mismatch the dark background theme (`#0D0D0D`).

---

### 3. iOS Input Auto-Zoom Quirk on Form Controls
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §4
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 857, line 493), `public/*.html`
- **Reason:** `.form-control` has `font-size: 0.95rem` (15.2px) and `.search-input` does not enforce minimum font size; iOS Safari automatically zooms into inputs with font size under 16px and leaves the viewport cropped and drifted.

---

### 4. Tap Highlight Flash on Touch Devices
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §2
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 73)
- **Reason:** `html` lacks `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent`, causing default gray/blue tap flashes on touch interactions that degrade the native feel.

---

### 5. Font Inflation in Landscape Orientation
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native Baseline
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 73)
- **Reason:** `html` lacks `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%`, allowing mobile WebKit to unpredictably inflate font sizes when the phone is rotated into landscape mode.

---

### 6. Tap Latency & Unintended Double-Tap Zoom Delay
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §5
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 223)
- **Reason:** Buttons, links, and interactive controls lack `touch-action: manipulation`, causing browsers to wait up to 300ms on tap to detect potential double-taps.

---

### 7. Long-Press Accidental Text Selection on Buttons & Controls
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §8
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 223, line 1126, line 320)
- **Reason:** Interactive controls (`.btn`, `.fab-register`, `.badge`, `.role-option`, `.navbar__toggle`) lack `user-select: none; -webkit-user-select: none; -webkit-touch-callout: none`, causing accidental text selection and iOS context callouts on long-press.

---

### 8. Sticky Hover State Trap on Touch Devices
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §1
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (lines 184, 247, 260, 272, 283, 294, 1149, 1248, 1298)
- **Reason:** Hover states (`:hover`) across buttons, links, cards, and FAB are ungated, remaining stuck in their hover appearance after being tapped on touchscreens until tapped elsewhere.

---

### 9. Lack of Immediate Touch Press Feedback (`:active`)
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / R-39 (Interaction Affordances) / mobile-native §5
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 223, line 1126)
- **Reason:** Buttons and interactive controls lack `:active` styles (`transform: scale(0.97)` / instant feedback), making taps feel unresponsive because changes only trigger on finger release instead of touch contact.

---

### 10. Sticky Navbar and Floating Action Button (FAB) Lack Safe Area Insets
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §7
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 118, line 1126)
- **Reason:** Sticky `.navbar` lacks `padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top)` for device notches/Dynamic Island, and `.fab-register` lacks `bottom: calc(20px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))` and right safe area, causing it to overlap the iOS Home indicator bar.

---

### 11. Root Overscroll Rubber-Banding and Modal Scroll Leaks
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §6
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 73, line 818)
- **Reason:** Missing `overscroll-behavior: none` on `html, body` and missing `overscroll-behavior: contain` on `.modal__body`, allowing modal internal scrolling to chain to and rubber-band the background page.

---

### 12. Modal Height Overflow When Virtual Keyboard Opens
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / mobile-native §3
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 820)
- **Reason:** `.modal__body` uses `max-height: 75vh` instead of dynamic viewport sizing (`75dvh`), which causes modal inputs (such as the report modal textarea) and submit buttons to be pushed offscreen when the mobile soft keyboard appears.

---

### 13. Under-Sized Mobile Tap Targets (< 44px) on Key Navigation and Action Controls
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / antislop-layoutmobile Tap Targets
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (line 210, line 805), `public/search.html` (line 114)
- **Reason:** Hamburger toggle button `.navbar__toggle` and modal close button `.modal__close` lack explicit width, height, or padding to satisfy the 44x44px minimum tap target, making them difficult to hit reliably with a thumb.

---

### 14. Mobile Navigation Header Collision & Missing Auth Controls in Dropdown
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / antislop-layoutmobile Mobile Navigation
- **Files:** `public/css/style.css` (lines 1091–1120), `public/js/api.js` (line 98, line 245), `public/*.html`

### 15. Uncontained Data Tables Leaking Horizontal Overflow
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / antislop-layoutmobile Horizontal Scroll Leak
- **Files:** `public/admin.html` (lines 122, 149), `public/dashboard-band.html` (lines 83, 114, 142), `public/dashboard-organizer.html` (line 68), `public/css/style.css` (line 980)

### 16. Section Header Collision on Mobile Viewports
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Rule Violated:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) / antislop-layoutmobile Grids & Stacking

- **Reason:** `.section-header` uses `display: flex; align-items: flex-end; justify-content: space-between;` without `flex-wrap: wrap;` or mobile column stacking, causing page titles and action buttons (e.g., "+ Posting Jadwal Baru") to collide or squeeze uncomfortably on small screens.

### 18. Mobile Form Virtual Keyboard Optimization Deficiencies
